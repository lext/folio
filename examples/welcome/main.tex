\section{Make room for mathematics}
The beauty of a good equation is how much it leaves unsaid:
\begin{equation}
  e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0.
\end{equation}
For a continuous function, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives
\[
  \int_a^b f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = F(b) - F(a).
\]
